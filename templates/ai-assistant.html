Password Security Tips

Best Practices

🔐 Use Strong Passwords

Combine uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. Aim for at least 12 characters.

🚫 Avoid Password Reuse

Use unique passwords for each account to prevent domino-effect breaches.

🔄 Regular Updates

Change passwords every 3-6 months, especially for important accounts.

📱 Enable 2FA

Add two-factor authentication to critical accounts for extra protection.

🛡️ Monitor Breaches

Regularly check if your passwords have been compromised in data breaches.
